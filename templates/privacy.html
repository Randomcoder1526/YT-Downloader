Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 5, 2026

1. Overview

This policy describes how PLAY (“the service”) handles information when you use a self-hosted or deployed instance. Because setups differ, your operator may layer additional practices; ask them if this copy is not specific enough.

2. Information processed

When you submit a YouTube URL, the server uses it to request metadata and media through yt-dlp. That may include titles, thumbnails, and stream URLs as provided by the platform.

To keep the service stable, the app may process:

  • Your IP address (for example, for basic rate limiting on API routes)
  • Temporary job identifiers and download filenames stored on disk until they expire

We do not sell personal data. This project does not include third-party advertising trackers in its default templates.

3. Retention

Download jobs and files are intended to be short-lived. Completed jobs expire after a configured time window; files associated with those jobs should be removed when jobs are cleaned up. Exact timing depends on your server configuration.

4. Third-party services

Requests go to YouTube (or other sites yt-dlp supports) according to the URL you provide. Those services have their own privacy policies and terms. PLAY is not endorsed by or affiliated with YouTube or Google.

5. Security

Self-hosted instances are only as secure as the host, network, and configuration you apply. Use HTTPS in production, restrict access if the app is not meant to be public, and keep dependencies updated.

6. Your choices

If you are an end user of someone else’s deployment, contact that operator for access, correction, or deletion requests. If you run the software yourself, you control the data on your machine.

7. Changes

We may update this page as the project evolves. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when material edits are made.

8. Contact

For privacy questions about a specific live site, contact the person or organization operating that instance. For general project inquiries, use the contact page.
