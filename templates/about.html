Our story

Built for clarity, speed, and control

PLAY is a focused interface for fetching and downloading YouTube playlists and individual videos in formats you choose.

What we are

PLAY is a web application that combines a Flask backend with yt-dlp to retrieve metadata and media. The goal is a clean workflow: paste a link, pick MP4 or MP3, and download—without clutter.

Playlist mode helps you review a list of videos, select what you need, and run parallel downloads. Single mode streamlines the same pipeline for one URL.

How we think about use

We expect you to respect copyright, platform terms, and local law. PLAY is a tool; how you use it is your responsibility. Many creators allow offline access through official apps—when in doubt, prefer those channels.

Stack

  • Python, Flask, and yt-dlp on the server
  • FFmpeg for audio encoding and container work where needed
  • A minimal, responsive front end—no heavy frameworks required
